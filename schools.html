FOR TEACHERS & SCHOOL IT

Xyvora at school.

What Xyvora does, what it shares, and what your school can review before allowing access.

If your school blocks Xyvora

Ask a teacher or your school’s IT team to review https://xyvora.net/ for the class activity. Include the exact error or filter category and the intended use. Remove names, student data, and account details from screenshots.

Only your school can decide whether to allow access on its network. Follow the school’s AI and internet-use rules.

Network and browser requirements

Website
https://xyvora.net/ (HTTPS). The About and Schools pages work without JavaScript.
Basic chat
A modern browser with JavaScript and streaming HTTPS responses to the same site, including /api/chat. No account, browser extension, or model download is required.
Optional sign-in
Basic chat and built-in tools work without an account. The PDF reader loads extra assets only when opened.

For an approved test, open the homepage and ask “What is 18% of 84.50?” to check a browser tool, then ask for a short explanation of a class topic to check a streamed server reply.

Student privacy and appropriate use

  • Don’t enter student records, contact details, grades, or other sensitive information. For server replies, Xyvora receives the prompt and can receive recent context and extracted attachment text.
  • Guest chat history is kept in the current browser profile. Signed-in history can sync to an account. On a shared device, use the school’s approved setup and clear guest history or sign out when finished.
  • Web search sends the requested query to outside search services. Built-in tools such as the calculator run on the device. Signed-in accounts can sync chats and settings when enabled.
  • Use Xyvora for explanations, practice, and feedback when allowed. Verify answers and follow your teacher’s instructions for assignments.
