What you can do

Choose Instant for a short answer, or switch to Normal, Thinker, Pro, or Creative for longer work. Ask for help learning a concept, explore a file, use built-in tools, or review an action in Plugins.

Basic chat does not require an account. Guest history is saved in your browser. Signed-in accounts can sync saved chats, settings, and memory. Chat can send your prompt, recent context, and extracted attachment text to the server; optional external tools tell you what they will share before you run them.
