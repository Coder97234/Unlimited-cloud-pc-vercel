HOW WE COUNT

Browsers, not people

Activity is estimated from recent heartbeats by opted-in browsers. Two tabs on the same browser count once. Devices and cleared storage can count separately.

“Returning” means the same browser was active on at least two UTC days in the last 30 days. Account totals are separate; activity isn’t linked to identity.
